# LixBlogs outreach — write

For reaching out to someone with an idea, draft or story worth publishing.

## Prompt

A 16:9 social-card background design (1280x720) in editorial tech-minimalism — structured, clever, airy and a little playful. Flat 2D vector only: NO 3D, NO gradients, NO glow, NO neon, NO shadows.

CRITICAL: NO TEXT — no words, letters, numbers or typography anywhere, EXCEPT the single letter "E" inside Oreo's small chest badge. Leave the entire LEFT ~55% as clean, almost-empty negative space for typography added later. Place ALL artwork in the RIGHT ~40-42%, pushed toward the right edge with a clear gap before the text zone.

Background: pure white #ffffff covered edge-to-edge by a faint, precise matrix of tiny evenly spaced #d9d9dd dots, subtle and receding.

Mascot and action: Oreo the panda, seated and leaning forward with cheerful concentration, writes with one simple oversized fountain pen on a large floating blank page angled gently upward. The pen, paw and page are all integrated into one elegant visual gesture. Oreo is a TRUE continuous single-line drawing: ONE unbroken, flowing, very thin #212121 stroke with only a few intentional loops. Cute, round and plump in shape; face suggested with minimal sparkling eyes and a small smile; head turned slightly LEFT toward the empty headline area. No realistic rendering, solid black patches, shading or smooth cartoon treatment. Both ears and one leg are flat coral #ff7759. A small compact perfectly round red #dc3c32 badge is centred on the chest and contains one bold stylish letter "E". Those are the only filled areas.

Supporting geometry: the blank page is a thin #d9d9dd outline with three short abstract content lines that contain no characters; add one open circle and one loose arc around it, also #d9d9dd hairline. Keep the objects sparse, slightly oversized and compositionally funky, never cluttered. The artwork must remain completely clear of the left text zone.

Constraints: one Oreo only; one continuous fine ink line; exactly one letter in the entire image, the "E" badge; no other text or symbols resembling letters; no watermark; no crop. Saturated colour appears only on Oreo's coral ears, coral leg and red badge.
