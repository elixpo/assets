## Prompt

A 16:9 social-card background design (1280x720) in editorial tech-minimalism — structured, clever, airy and a little playful. Flat 2D vector only: NO 3D, NO gradients, NO glow, NO neon, NO shadows.

CRITICAL: NO TEXT — no words, letters, numbers or typography anywhere, EXCEPT the single letter "E" inside Oreo's small chest badge. Leave the entire LEFT ~55% as clean, almost-empty negative space for typography added later. Place ALL artwork in the RIGHT ~40-42%, pushed toward the right edge with a clear gap before the text zone.

Background: pure white #ffffff covered edge-to-edge by a faint, precise matrix of tiny evenly spaced #d9d9dd dots, subtle and receding.

Mascot and action: Oreo the panda joyfully launches a folded document-page paper plane upward and slightly to the right. The plane still subtly reads as a blog page through two short abstract content strokes, with no text or characters. Oreo's paw follows through and the head turns slightly LEFT toward the headline, giving the whole pose friendly momentum. Oreo is a TRUE continuous single-line drawing: ONE unbroken, flowing, very thin #212121 stroke with only a few gentle loops. Cute, round and plump; face minimally suggested with sparkling eyes and a small smile. No realistic rendering, solid black patches, shading or smooth cartoon treatment. Both ears and one leg are flat coral #ff7759. A small compact perfectly round red #dc3c32 chest badge contains one bold stylish letter "E". Those are the only filled areas.

Supporting geometry: the plane and its long curling flight path are thin #d9d9dd outlines, joined by one open circle and one small square along the route. The motion should feel buoyant and funky but remain clean and editorial. No envelope icon, share icon, arrows, labels or characters. Keep the entire composition on the right.

Constraints: one Oreo only; one continuous fine ink line; exactly one letter in the entire image, the "E" badge; no watermark; no crop. Saturated colour appears only on Oreo's coral ears, coral leg and red badge.
