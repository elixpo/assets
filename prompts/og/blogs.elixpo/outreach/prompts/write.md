# LixBlogs outreach — write

Reusable story illustration for the “That idea deserves a URL” social card.
The API generates only Oreo's artwork; Pillow supplies the tangy card, blobs,
dotted field and typography afterward.

## Prompt

Use case: ads-marketing.
Asset type: reusable transparent-style story illustration for an Elixpo Blogs outreach social card.

Create a single, compact editorial illustration on a perfectly flat pure white background. Show Oreo, the Elixpo panda, telling a tiny visual story about turning a loose idea into a written blog post.

STORY ACTION: one playful unbroken ink line begins as a small loose thought-loop above Oreo's head, curls down to form Oreo's head and plump seated body, flows through one paw into a short fountain pen, crosses a little blank page resting in Oreo's lap, then continues off the page and ends as a tiny paper-plane flourish. The eye should be able to follow the line from idea, to Oreo, to writing, to sharing. Make the pose lively and curious, with a slight forward lean and a pleased little smile. This must feel like a narrative captured in one stroke, not a static mascot sitting beside random props.

LINE-ART LOCK: Oreo and the story gesture are a TRUE continuous-line drawing made from ONE unbroken, very thin, single-weight #212121 stroke. Use loose calligraphic loops, cheeky asymmetry and a few surprising overlaps so the line feels funky and hand-drawn, while keeping Oreo instantly readable as a cute panda. The face is suggested with the fewest possible marks: two small gleaming eyes with tiny catch-lights and a little smile. No solid black eye patches, no heavy outline, no filled black limbs, no detailed face, no shading, no realistic rendering and no smooth conventional cartoon. The line itself is the hero.

OREO IDENTITY: round healthy panda proportions; head angled slightly left; both round ears filled flat coral #ff7759; one small leg patch filled flat coral #ff7759. A small compact perfectly round red #dc3c32 badge is centred on the chest and carries one clean bold stylish letter "E". The coral areas and red badge are the only filled colour. The "E" is the only letter anywhere.

COMPOSITION: one self-contained square vignette, centered, with the complete story comfortably inside the middle 72% of the image and generous blank white padding around every edge. The small page stays in Oreo's lap. The short pen stays close to the body. The thought-loop and paper-plane ending remain close enough that the complete silhouette is compact and easy to place on a social card. No desk and no large foreground paper.

BACKGROUND FOR EXTRACTION: perfectly uniform pure white #ffffff, edge to edge, with no dots, blobs, texture, shadows, floor, gradients, lighting variation or geometry. The subject must not touch any edge. Crisp clean edges suitable for corner flood-fill background removal.

Constraints: exactly one Oreo; exactly one continuous fine story line; exactly one letter total, the "E" on the badge; no other typography, numbers, watermark, logo, UI, border or crop. Do not create a polished filled panda illustration. Do not add extra characters or disconnected props.
