# LixBlogs outreach idea — publish

For showing how a draft becomes a live page without making publishing feel technical.

## Prompt

A 16:9 social-card background design (1280x720) in editorial tech-minimalism — structured, clever, airy and a little playful. Flat 2D vector only: NO 3D, NO gradients, NO glow, NO neon, NO shadows.

CRITICAL: NO TEXT — no words, letters, numbers or typography anywhere, EXCEPT the single letter "E" inside Oreo's small chest badge. Leave the entire LEFT ~55% as clean, almost-empty negative space for typography added later. Place ALL artwork in the RIGHT ~40-42%, pushed toward the right edge with a clear gap before the text zone.

Background: pure white #ffffff covered edge-to-edge by a faint, precise matrix of tiny evenly spaced #d9d9dd dots, subtle and receding.

Mascot and action: Oreo the panda sits beside a simple open circular portal, happily guiding a blank document page through it; on the other side the page emerges as a clean browser-window outline with no controls, text or characters. The transformation reads immediately as draft-to-live, using only outlines. Oreo is a TRUE continuous single-line drawing: ONE unbroken, flowing, very thin #212121 stroke with a few deliberate loops. Cute, round and plump in shape; minimally suggested sparkling eyes and a little smile; head turned slightly LEFT. No realistic rendering, solid black patches, shading or smooth cartoon treatment. Both ears and one leg are flat coral #ff7759. A small compact perfectly round red #dc3c32 chest badge contains one bold stylish letter "E". Those are the only filled areas.

Supporting geometry: one large open portal circle, one page outline and one browser-window outline, all thin #d9d9dd hairline; a short curved motion trail connects the page states. Keep the shapes sparse and playful like a precise design-system wireframe. No labels, icons, UI text or extra marks that resemble letters. Keep everything on the right and clear of the left text zone.

Constraints: one Oreo only; one continuous fine ink line; exactly one letter in the entire image, the "E" badge; no watermark; no crop. Saturated colour appears only on Oreo's coral ears, coral leg and red badge.
