# LixBlogs outreach idea — collaborate

For inviting a co-writer, editor or teammate into the same story.

## Prompt

A 16:9 social-card background design (1280x720) in editorial tech-minimalism — structured, clever, airy and a little playful. Flat 2D vector only: NO 3D, NO gradients, NO glow, NO neon, NO shadows.

CRITICAL: NO TEXT — no words, letters, numbers or typography anywhere, EXCEPT the single letter "E" inside Oreo's small chest badge. Leave the entire LEFT ~55% as clean, almost-empty negative space for typography added later. Place ALL artwork in the RIGHT ~40-42%, pushed toward the right edge with a clear gap before the text zone.

Background: pure white #ffffff covered edge-to-edge by a faint, precise matrix of tiny evenly spaced #d9d9dd dots, subtle and receding.

Mascot and action: Oreo the panda happily fits two floating blank page fragments together like puzzle pieces, creating one complete document page. Two tiny abstract cursor triangles approach from different directions, implying two collaborators without adding another character. Oreo is a TRUE continuous single-line drawing: ONE unbroken, flowing, very thin #212121 stroke with only a few balanced loops. Cute, round and plump; face minimally suggested with sparkling eyes and a little smile; head turned slightly LEFT. No realistic rendering, solid black patches, shading or smooth cartoon treatment. Both ears and one leg are flat coral #ff7759. A small compact perfectly round red #dc3c32 chest badge contains one bold stylish letter "E". Those are the only filled areas.

Supporting geometry: two joining page fragments, two cursor triangles and one open circle, all thin #d9d9dd hairline outlines. The assembled document may contain three short abstract horizontal strokes, but no text, characters, avatars, profile images or UI labels. Keep it clever, sparse, easy to read and entirely on the right.

Constraints: one Oreo only; one continuous fine ink line; exactly one letter in the entire image, the "E" badge; no watermark; no crop. Saturated colour appears only on Oreo's coral ears, coral leg and red badge.
