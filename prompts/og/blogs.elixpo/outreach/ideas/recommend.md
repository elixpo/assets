# LixBlogs outreach idea — recommend

For directly suggesting LixBlogs to someone who should be publishing their work.

## Prompt

A 16:9 social-card background design (1280x720) in editorial tech-minimalism — structured, clever, airy and a little playful. Flat 2D vector only: NO 3D, NO gradients, NO glow, NO neon, NO shadows.

CRITICAL: NO TEXT — no words, letters, numbers or typography anywhere, EXCEPT the single letter "E" inside Oreo's small chest badge. Leave the entire LEFT ~55% as clean, almost-empty negative space for typography added later. Place ALL artwork in the RIGHT ~40-42%, pushed toward the right edge with a clear gap before the text zone.

Background: pure white #ffffff covered edge-to-edge by a faint, precise matrix of tiny evenly spaced #d9d9dd dots, subtle and receding.

Mascot and action: Oreo the panda sits beside an inviting oversized browser-shaped doorway, presenting it with one open paw like a friendly personal recommendation. A blank page floats just inside the doorway, and a thin path curves toward it. Oreo looks pleased and encouraging, head turned slightly LEFT toward the person reading the card. Oreo is a TRUE continuous single-line drawing: ONE unbroken, flowing, very thin #212121 stroke with only a few intentional loops. Cute, round and plump; face minimally suggested with sparkling eyes and a small smile. No realistic rendering, solid black patches, shading or smooth cartoon treatment. Both ears and one leg are flat coral #ff7759. A small compact perfectly round red #dc3c32 chest badge contains one bold stylish letter "E". Those are the only filled areas.

Supporting geometry: browser doorway, blank page, open circle and curved path are thin #d9d9dd hairline outlines. The browser has no buttons, labels, address bar content or text-like marks. Keep the composition whimsical but precise, sparse, and entirely inside the right-side art zone.

Constraints: one Oreo only; one continuous fine ink line; exactly one letter in the entire image, the "E" badge; no pointing finger; no watermark; no crop. Saturated colour appears only on Oreo's coral ears, coral leg and red badge.
