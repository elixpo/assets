# LixBlogs outreach idea — audience

For showing that one useful post can travel far beyond its first reader.

## Prompt

A 16:9 social-card background design (1280x720) in editorial tech-minimalism — structured, clever, airy and a little playful. Flat 2D vector only: NO 3D, NO gradients, NO glow, NO neon, NO shadows.

CRITICAL: NO TEXT — no words, letters, numbers or typography anywhere, EXCEPT the single letter "E" inside Oreo's small chest badge. Leave the entire LEFT ~55% as clean, almost-empty negative space for typography added later. Place ALL artwork in the RIGHT ~40-42%, pushed toward the right edge with a clear gap before the text zone.

Background: pure white #ffffff covered edge-to-edge by a faint, precise matrix of tiny evenly spaced #d9d9dd dots, subtle and receding.

Mascot and action: Oreo the panda holds up one small blank page while watching its idea ripple outward through three increasingly large open circles. Along the circles, two tiny blank page outlines appear farther away, suggesting the post reaching new readers. Oreo looks quietly delighted, head turned slightly LEFT toward the headline. Oreo is a TRUE continuous single-line drawing: ONE unbroken, flowing, very thin #212121 stroke with only a few intentional loops. Cute, round and plump; face minimally suggested with sparkling eyes and a small smile. No realistic rendering, solid black patches, shading or smooth cartoon treatment. Both ears and one leg are flat coral #ff7759. A small compact perfectly round red #dc3c32 chest badge contains one bold stylish letter "E". Those are the only filled areas.

Supporting geometry: three open ripple circles and three blank document outlines in total, all thin #d9d9dd hairline. The pages contain no text, characters or icons. The ripple network should feel expansive and a little surprising, but stay sparse and clear of the left text zone.

Constraints: one Oreo only; one continuous fine ink line; exactly one letter in the entire image, the "E" badge; no people, audience silhouettes, social-media logos, watermark or crop. Saturated colour appears only on Oreo's coral ears, coral leg and red badge.
