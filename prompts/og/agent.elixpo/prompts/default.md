# Agent Elixpo — default OG card

Canonical social card for the autonomous Elixpo contributor agent.

## Prompt

A 16:9 social-card background design (1280x720) in editorial tech-minimalism —
clean corporate design meets a modern open-source engineering portfolio.
Structured, intellectual and airy. Flat 2D vector only: NO 3D, NO gradients,
NO glow, NO neon, NO shadows.

CRITICAL: NO TEXT — no words, letters, numbers or typography anywhere, EXCEPT
the single letter "E" inside the panda's small chest badge. Create only one
isolated mascot-and-infographic cluster, fully visible with generous padding.
Pillow will resize and position the cluster later, so do not create a scene,
frame, panel, floor, border or typography.

Background: perfectly flat solid pure white #ffffff for local background
removal. No dots, texture, lighting, shadows or decoration may touch the outer
background. Keep the artwork separated from every canvas edge.

Mascot — Oreo as a TRUE single-line drawing: ONE unbroken, flowing,
hand-drawn VERY THIN #212121 line, like a single pen stroke that never lifts,
with only a few gentle loops — loose, minimal and elegant. This is casual LINE
ART, NOT a proper polished Oreo figure, accurately-rendered mascot, filled
illustration or smooth cartoon. Do not construct a large complete character
from clean closed shapes. The line casually suggests a cute, friendly panda,
round and plump in shape. The loose single-line treatment applies to the outer
head and body; draw the tiny facial features as separate, controlled marks so
the expression remains clean. Keep the face unobstructed; any deliberate
crossover loops stay around the outer body, never across the eyes or mouth.
Oreo turns slightly LEFT toward the text.

EXPRESSION LOCK: Oreo looks calm, alert, warm and quietly confident. Draw two
small matching vertical oval eyes, equal in size and placed on the same
horizontal axis. Put one tiny white catch-light in each eye at the same relative
position. Both pupils point gently toward the LEFT and remain perfectly
aligned. Add one tiny centred black nose and one short, closed, upward-curving
smile directly beneath it. Keep the head level and the facial spacing balanced.
No crossed eyes, rolling pupils, mismatched eye sizes, uneven gaze, drooping or
sleepy eyelids, spiral eyes, dazed expression, open mouth, tongue, smirk,
extreme grin, tilted face, manic excitement, confusion or intoxicated look.

Both ears and one leg are filled flat coral #ff7759. Centred on the suggested
chest is a SMALL compact round red #DC3C32 badge carrying one clean cream "E".
These are the only filled areas; the rest of Oreo remains the thin #212121
single-line sketch. No extra arms, held tools, thick outlines, black body
fills, shading, detailed anatomy or sticker border.

LEG LOCK: show exactly two distinct seated legs, both fully visible and clearly
separated. One leg is the flat coral leg; the other is cream/white and defined
only by the thin ink contour. Neither leg may be hidden, merged into the belly,
cropped, omitted or replaced by a trailing line.

Geometry: add exactly three understated agent-workflow shapes in extremely
light, thin single-weight #d9d9dd hairline outlines: one blank issue/page
outline, one simple three-node branch, and one small completion-check circle.
Keep them pale and quiet. Arrange them compactly behind and around Oreo; never
use dark ink, coral or red on them and never fill them.

Colour: coral #ff7759 on both ears and one leg, and red #DC3C32 on the small E
badge, are the only saturated colours. Everything else is #212121 ink and
#d9d9dd hairline on white. No wrench, GitHub logo, Octocat, UI, browser,
laptop, robot, brain, generic AI symbol, third-party branding or watermark.
