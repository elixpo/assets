# Elixpo LinkedIn page copy

## Tagline

An open-source ecosystem built in public by the next generation of developers.

## About

Elixpo is an open-source ecosystem built as a university project series by a
group of teen developers who believe the best way to learn technology is to
build it, ship it and share the journey.

What began as a collection of ambitious experiments is growing into a connected
ecosystem of creative tools, publishing platforms and developer-first products.
Every Elixpo project is shaped by curiosity, practical learning and a commitment
to building useful technology in the open.

We are Gen Z builders: experimental by nature, community-first by choice and
unafraid to turn unusual ideas into working products. We document what we learn,
welcome thoughtful contributions and believe young developers deserve the space
to build things that are real—not just theoretical.

Now we are taking Elixpo beyond our own screens. We want to connect with
students, developers, designers, mentors, open-source communities, institutions
and organisations that care about learning through creation and making
technology more open, playful and accessible.

Follow our builds, explore the ecosystem, contribute to a project or start a
conversation with us. There is room here for anyone curious enough to make
something meaningful.

Built by young developers. Built in the open. Built for everyone.
