## Prompt

Create a square 1:1 brand-avatar artwork for the official Elixpo LinkedIn page.
Elixpo is an open-source ecosystem built in public by teen developers. The
image must feel like a clean, credible developer-community identity with a
playful Gen-Z Tangy edge. Flat 2D vector style only.

Place Oreo, Elixpo's friendly round panda mascot, large and centered inside a
circle-safe region covering no more than 76% of the canvas. Oreo uses minimal
fine #212121 line art, two flat coral #ff7759 ears, one coral lower-leg shape,
and a compact round #dc3c32 chest badge containing one clean white E. Oreo must
remain instantly recognizable at 32px and survive a circular profile crop with
no clipped ears, face, or badge.

Behind Oreo create one restrained Tangy halo: an asymmetric peach #ffdec7
organic blob, one small sunny-yellow #ffdd70 circle, one short coral #ff7759
capsule, and two thin #212121 editorial arcs suggesting curiosity and momentum.
Use a warm-white #fffcf7 canvas with an extremely subtle evenly spaced #d9d9dd
dotted matrix.

No words, captions, slogans, numbers, or extra letters. The white E inside
Oreo's chest badge is the only permitted text. No third-party logos, border,
mockup, watermark, gradients, 3D, glow, shadows, photorealism, stock characters,
or glossy cartoon rendering.
