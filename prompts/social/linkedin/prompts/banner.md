## Prompt

Create an ultra-wide 4:1 LinkedIn page banner for Elixpo, an open-source
ecosystem built as a university project series by teen developers. Communicate
curiosity, collaboration, practical building, and open-source momentum. The
style is credible developer-community editorial design with youthful Tangy
energy: intelligent and confident, never childish. Flat 2D vector only.

Use a warm-white #fffcf7 background with a faint edge-to-edge #d9d9dd dotted
matrix. Build a left-to-right story with one fine #212121 line: a loose code
bracket flows into connected nodes, then a small sprout, a paper plane, and an
open path continuing beyond the right edge. The visual narrative is idea,
build, share, grow.

Keep the entire left 58% calm and nearly empty so the approved Elixpo wordmark
and page copy can be composited later. Reserve the lower-left corner for the
LinkedIn page-avatar overlap. Place Oreo in the right 30%, facing left. Oreo is
a round friendly panda in minimal #212121 line art with coral #ff7759 ears and
one leg, plus a small round #dc3c32 chest badge containing one white E. Oreo
guides a small coral paper plane into the open path.

Behind Oreo add one large pale-peach #ffdec7 organic blob, one blush #ffcdd3
pill, one sunny-yellow #ffdd70 circle, and two short coral #ff7759 motion marks.
Allow some shapes to peek beyond the top and right edges while keeping Oreo's
face and badge crisp.

Render no wordmark, headline, slogan, caption, code, numbers, or extra letters.
The E badge is the only permitted letter. No UI screenshot, fake browser,
laptop mockup, third-party logo, watermark, gradient, 3D, glow, shadow,
photorealism, or stock character.
